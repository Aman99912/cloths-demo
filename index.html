The Brand

About AAVRA

We are an independent Indian clothing label exploring the space between heritage and modernity. Our collections are built around thoughtful proportions, tactile fabrics and details that reveal themselves slowly.

Founded with the belief that clothing should feel as considered as it looks, AAVRA works with a small network of artisans across Rajasthan and Gujarat, combining their mastery with contemporary design to create pieces that belong in the modern wardrobe.
